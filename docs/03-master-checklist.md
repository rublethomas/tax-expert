# Master checklist (everything to collect, per tax year)

## Identity / status
- [ ] Passports, TN approvals (I-94 records), I-797s, entry/exit dates (CBP I-94 website)
- [ ] SSNs, SINs; Canadian & US addresses; direct-deposit info
- [ ] Day-count worksheet (US/Canada days per year) → substantial presence test

## US – salary (both)
- [ ] W-2 Amex; W-2 GSPANN (due to you by Jan 31)
- [ ] Final pay stubs (check withholding, 401k, HSA, imputed income)
- [ ] 1099-INT/DIV/B, 1099-R, 5498, 1095-A/B/C, stock plan statements (RSU/ESPP)
- [ ] Prior-year US return (if any)

## US – foreign items
- [ ] Canadian rental: gross rent in CAD → USD (IRS yearly average rate), expenses, mortgage interest, property tax, purchase price & date (depreciable basis, land vs building split) → Schedule E, Form 4562
- [ ] Canadian tax paid on rent (NR4 box / s.216 assessment) → Form 1116
- [ ] Canadian dividends: dividend slips/NR4, withholding (15%) → Schedule B, Form 1116
- [ ] FBAR (FinCEN 114): max balance in every foreign account, incl. corporation account signing authority
- [ ] Form 8938 if above threshold
- [ ] Form 5471 (corporation financials in USD, shareholder info, Schedule E/G/I etc.) – CPA recommended
- [ ] TFSA/RESP/FHSA review (Form 3520/3520-A?)
- [ ] Form 8833 treaty disclosure if taking treaty positions (e.g., tie-breaker)
- [ ] Estimated tax payments? (Q1–Q4 vouchers if rent/dividend income not covered by withholding)

## Canada – non-resident rental
- [ ] Title documents & ownership %; property agent agreement
- [ ] NR6 (file before first rent of year; estimate net rent)
- [ ] 25% withholding remittances (NR76 / agent), NR4 slips by Mar 31
- [ ] Rent ledger, expense receipts (CAD), mortgage statements
- [ ] Section 216 return (T1159 / non-resident T1) by Jun 30; CRA My Account / Represent a Client
- [ ] Provincial tax: Ontario property → Ontario rate applies in s.216 return

## Canada – existing corporation dividends
- [ ] Corporation resolution/dividend declaration, amounts, dates
- [ ] Part XIII withholding remitted (by 15th of next month) and NR4 filed (Mar 31)
- [ ] Corporation T2 filed (6 months after YE) and balance owing paid (2–3 months after YE)
- [ ] Corporate year-end financial statements / trial balance in CAD (needed for 5471)
- [ ] Confirm corporation's tax residency and any impact of an owner living abroad (central management & control)

## Next year – new Ontario incorporation (planning)
- [ ] Immigration attorney sign-off on TN compatibility (blocking item)
- [ ] Incorporate (Ontario/federal), CRA business number, HST/GST registration if > $30k
- [ ] Corporate bank account (note FBAR/5471 implications), bookkeeping setup, accountant
- [ ] Shareholder agreement; how you are paid (dividend vs salary) and where services are performed
- [ ] US reporting: Form 5471 from year of formation; CFC/GILTI analysis; consider alternatives with CPA
- [ ] Annual: T2, Ontario annual return, NR4s, Part XIII remittances

## Year-end
- [ ] Reconcile all income to slips; FX conversion log
- [ ] Keep copies 7 years (US: 3–6 years; foreign-form errors can keep the statute open indefinitely)
- [ ] Update checklist.md and notes.md for next year
