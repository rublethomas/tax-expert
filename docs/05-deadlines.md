# Deadline calendar (verify each year on irs.gov / canada.ca)

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Before first rent of year (generally by Dec 31) | **NR6** for the next year | Canada rental |
| 15th of month after payment | Part XIII remittance (rent withholding, dividend withholding) | Agent / corporation |
| Jan 31 | W-2s issued | Employers |
| Mar 31 | NR4 slips issued; NR4 summary filed | Agent / corporation |
| Apr 15 | US Form 1040 (MFJ) + FBAR due (auto-extended to Oct 15); Form 5471 attached to 1040 | US |
| Apr 15 / Jun 15 / Sep 15 / Jan 15 | US estimated tax if needed | US |
| Jun 30 | **Section 216 return** (non-resident rental, with NR6) | Canada |
| Oct 15 | US extended 1040 deadline (extension to file, not to pay) | US |
| 6 months after corp year-end | Corporate T2 filing; balance due 2–3 months after YE | Corporation |
| Apr 30 / Dec 31 (rule dependent) | Other s.216 timing rules – see CRA page | Canada |
