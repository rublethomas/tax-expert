1. Read CLAUDE.md and docs/02-open-questions.md.
2. Ask the unanswered questions in small groups (A→F); write answers inline in that file.
3. Compute a preliminary view: US substantial-presence status from the day counts (current + 1/3 prior + 1/6 second-prior, need 31 days current and 183 weighted), Canadian residency ties, and which forms in docs/03-master-checklist.md apply (5471, FBAR, 8938, NR6, s.216, Part XIII).
4. Record conclusions with dates in years/<YYYY>/notes.md and flag items needing CPA/immigration-lawyer review. Do not store SSN/SIN/account numbers.
