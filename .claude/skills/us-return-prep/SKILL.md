name: us-return-prep
description: Prepare US federal return worksheets (1040 data, Schedule E for Canadian rental with depreciation, Schedule B, Form 1116, FBAR/8938 data) for a tax year. Use when preparing or reviewing the US filing.
---
1. Read years/<YYYY>/checklist.md and notes.md; list missing documents first.
2. Build worksheets in years/<YYYY>/us/worksheets/ (markdown or xlsx): W-2 summary; Schedule E (rent CAD→USD at IRS yearly average rate, expenses, depreciation: residential 27.5-year straight-line, mid-month convention, land excluded, USD basis at acquisition rate); Schedule B (dividends, FX); Form 1116 (Canadian tax by income category); FBAR/8938 max-balance table.
3. State every assumption and cite IRS pages (Pub 519, Pub 527, Form instructions) for the tax year; verify current numbers via web search rather than memory.
4. Output a "data-entry sheet" in the order of the chosen software. Flag 5471/treaty items for CPA. Never file.
